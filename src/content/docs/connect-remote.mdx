title: Connect from Claude.ai / ChatGPT
description: Add Health Data Safe as a connector in Claude.ai, ChatGPT or Claude Code. Nothing to install; sign in on HDS and choose what your assistant may read and write.
---

You can connect your AI assistant to your Health Data Safe (HDS) account
without installing anything on your computer. You add HDS as a **connector**
in your assistant's settings, then sign in on HDS.

## 1. Get your connector address

Sign in to your HDS account and open the **AI assistant** tab
([account.hds.ngo/account/ai-assistant](https://account.hds.ngo/account/ai-assistant)).
It shows the connector address for your HDS region and the client ID for each
assistant, with copy buttons. The address depends on where your data is
hosted, so always take it from that tab.

For reference, the addresses are:

| Region      | Connector address                                  |
|-------------|----------------------------------------------------|
| Switzerland | `https://auth-ch.api.datasafe.dev/adapter/mcp/mcp` |
| USA         | `https://auth-us.api.datasafe.dev/adapter/mcp/mcp` |

## 2. Add the connector

**Claude.ai:** Settings > Connectors > **Add custom connector**. Paste the
connector address, open the advanced settings and enter the OAuth Client ID
`hds-mcp-claude`. Leave the client secret empty.

**ChatGPT:** in its connector settings, add a custom connector with the same
address and the Client ID `hds-mcp-chatgpt`, no secret.

**No client ID field, or not sure?** Add the address ending in `/raw` instead
of `/mcp` (also on the AI assistant tab). Your assistant will give you a
sign-in link to open; the connection lasts 4 hours and covers all your data
except genetic results.

**Claude Code:** in a terminal, with your connector address in place of
`CONNECTOR_ADDRESS`,

```sh
claude mcp add --transport http --client-id hds-mcp-claude-code hds CONNECTOR_ADDRESS
claude mcp login hds
```

## 3. Sign in and choose what to share

Your assistant opens an HDS page. Sign in (or create an account), then read
the consent screen: it lists the kinds of data your assistant asks for.

- **Read-only at first.** The connector can only read what you approve.
- **Writing is asked theme by theme.** The first time your assistant wants to
  add data to a theme (for example Body), HDS asks you to allow it. Nothing is
  written until you accept.
- You can remove the connector at any time from your HDS account.

## What works differently from the installed server

- The connector works on your real (production) HDS account. To try it
  first, use the [installed server](/install/) with a demo account.
- Your assistant cannot create new data folders (streams) in your account.
  It can add data where your HDS apps already created them. For a large
  import that needs new ones, use the [installed server](/install/).
- **Files:** your assistant reads the documents you attach in the chat and
  records the values it finds in them. The original documents cannot be
  stored in HDS yet.

:::caution[Your assistant's provider sees what it reads]
Everything your assistant reads, from your HDS account or from the files you
attach, is sent to the company that runs it. A provider that does not train
on your data is the safer choice for medical records. You can stop at any
point.
:::
